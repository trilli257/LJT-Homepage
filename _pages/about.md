# Junteng Liu

I am a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, where I am advised by **Prof. Junxian He** in the HKUST NLP Group. My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning**.

I am broadly interested in the intersection of language models with reasoning, reward, and perception:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning** — studying how to develop and improve large language models' reasoning capabilities through reinforcement-based objectives and self-supervision.
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)** — investigating where and why vision-language models produce confident false or unsupported content, and how to detect or mitigate such errors.
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability** — exploring the internal mechanisms that underlie truthful responses, including whether structured "truthfulness" representations exist inside learned models.
